About UTAP

The Unified Technical Assessment Platform (UTAP) is an academic initiative designed to enhance and structure the recruitment evaluation process. It integrates resume screening, candidate assessment, and intelligent interview shortlisting into a unified workflow.

Our Vision

To establish a transparent and organized evaluation system that assists recruiters in identifying qualified candidates efficiently while ensuring fairness and clarity for applicants.

Problem Statement

Traditional hiring workflows often involve manual resume review, inconsistent evaluation standards, and lengthy shortlisting processes. These challenges can reduce efficiency and create ambiguity in decision-making.

Our Approach

UTAP connects resume evaluation, structured assessments, and final shortlisting into a cohesive system. By centralizing these processes, the platform ensures consistency, efficiency, and a streamlined recruitment experience.
